05 Mechanics

Two materials doing one job.

01 The suspension

Fine particles float in a viscous base between two sealed polycarbonate walls. Tilt the case and the field drifts, settles and re-forms. It is pressure-tested at the factory and never opened again.

02 The pigment

Strontium aluminate is bonded into the inner wall rather than sprayed on the surface, so abrasion never reaches it. It absorbs across the visible spectrum and re-emits at 505 nm — the wavelength the eye reads best in low light.

    07 Before you buy

    The five things people ask.

    Which fit do I need?

    Check the model number on the underside of your charging case and match it in the Fit section above. The four shells are tooled separately and are not interchangeable.

    Does it block wireless charging?

    No. The shell is under 2 mm and Qi passes straight through — drop it on the pad with the case on.

    How do I charge the glow?

    Any light source. Ten minutes of direct sun gives the fullest burn; a desk lamp or a phone torch held close for thirty seconds is enough for an evening.

    Does the glow wear out?

    The compound is inorganic and does not degrade with charge cycles. Sunlight will fade the printed colourway long before the glow weakens.

    Can the quicksand leak?

    The cavity is ultrasonically welded and pressure-tested before it leaves the line. It is not user-serviceable and should not be opened.
