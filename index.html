03 inside the shell

Two materials, one job.

  1. The suspension

    Fine particles float in a viscous base between two sealed polycarbonate walls. Tilt the case and the field drifts, settles and re-forms. It is pressure-tested at the factory and never opened again.

  2. The pigment

    Strontium aluminate is bonded into the inner wall rather than sprayed on the surface, so abrasion never reaches it. It absorbs across the visible spectrum and re-emits at 505 nm — the wavelength the eye reads best in low light.

08 before you buy

The awkward questions.

Will it fit my AirPods?

Four shells cover AirPods 1 & 2, Pro 1 & 2, AirPods 3 and AirPods 4. The generations share an outer shape within each pair, so one shell covers both. Pick your model in the fit section and the dimensions in the spec table update to match.

Is the six-hour glow real?

Six hours is the full taper, not six hours at full brightness. It is brightest in the first hour and readable in a dark room well past the fifth. Ten minutes under a lamp or direct daylight is enough to charge it.

Can I charge through it?

Yes — Qi pass-through works with the case on, and the LED window is left uncovered so you can still see the charge state.

What does shipping cost?
